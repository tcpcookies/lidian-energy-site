About Lidian Power

A LiFePO4 battery factory in Huizhou, Guangdong — building storage systems from cell to cabinet for buyers worldwide.

Who We Are

One Factory, the Whole System

Huizhou Lidian Power Co., Ltd. designs and manufactures lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) energy storage products: all-in-one powerwall units, outdoor cabinet systems, rack battery modules and high-voltage ESS cabinets.

Our Huizhou facility covers module assembly, cabinet integration, sheet-metal fabrication and full-system aging and safety testing. Because one factory controls the whole chain, buyers get stable quality, direct pricing and fast customization.

We work with distributors, EPC contractors, installers and brand owners across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia and beyond — as both an OEM manufacturer and a long-term engineering partner.

Manufacturing

From Sheet Metal to Shipment

STEP 01

In-House Metalwork

Laser cutting and CNC forming of enclosures and cabinets in our own workshop — full control over structure quality and custom designs.

STEP 02

Module Assembly & BMS

Grade-A LiFePO4 cells assembled into 51.2V modules with smart BMS — every module capacity-checked and cycle-tested before racking.

STEP 03

System Integration & Testing

Cabinets integrated with cooling, wiring and monitoring, then full-system aged and safety-tested before DG-certified packing.
