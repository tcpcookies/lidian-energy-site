FAQ

Buyer Questions, Answered

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ starts from 1 unit for sample evaluation and 5–10 units for trial orders. Mixed containers combining different models are welcome.

Can I put my own brand on the products?

Yes. We offer logo printing, custom enclosure colors, branded UI/labels and retail packing. OEM orders above agreed quantities carry no extra branding cost.

Are your batteries certified for export?

Our LiFePO4 systems are built with grade-A cells and come with CE marking, UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and transport declarations. Third-party test reports can be arranged per model and market.

How do payments work?

Standard terms are T/T with 30% deposit and balance against B/L copy. L/C at sight is available for orders above USD 50,000.

What is the typical lead time?

20–35 days after sample approval and deposit, depending on model and quantity. Peak season (August–November) may need an extra 5–10 days.

Can you ship lithium batteries by sea and air?

Yes. We ship Class 9 lithium battery cargo with UN38.3, proper DG packing and declarations. Sea freight (LCL/FCL) is routine; air freight is arranged case by case.

Do you support system customization?

Absolutely — capacity, voltage platform, inverter matching, cabinet size, communication protocols (RS485/CAN/Bluetooth) and monitoring software can all be tailored.

How can I get your latest catalog?

Email or message us on WhatsApp — we will send the full catalog with FOB prices and packing details within 24 hours.
